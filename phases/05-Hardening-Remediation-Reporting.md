# Phase 5: Hardening, Remediation & Reporting

Objective
- Apply mitigations, harden configurations, and produce final reports and playbooks to reduce the attack surface and improve organizational resilience.

Key Deliverables
- Remediation plans for gaps identified during exercises
- Hardened baseline images and GPO templates
- Updated incident response runbooks and playbooks
- Final report summarizing findings, metrics, and recommended next steps

Example Tasks
- Apply mitigations for privileged account misuse, insecure services, and weak configurations
- Retest detection coverage after remediation
- Produce a final executive summary and technical appendices for both teams
